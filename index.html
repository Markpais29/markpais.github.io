(01) — About

The short version fits on a form. The long version is more fun.

I'm a senior at Michigan State studying astrophysics with a data science minor, graduating May 2027, living in East Lansing. That's the form.

The rest of it: I sang long before I played anything — ensembles, performances, a couple of awards I don't bring up unless asked. Guitar came later and became the thing I reach for when I need to think. Coding and research came later still. I got in by cold-emailing professors and saying yes to things I wasn't qualified for yet, which is honestly still the strategy.

Most of what I love has the same shape: something messy and random-looking on the surface with a pattern buried in it, if you sit with it long enough. A recipe. A solo. A dataset. A projector currently in pieces on my desk.

(03) — The lab

Also, briefly

I run N-body simulations of planet formation in the Planetary Makerspace Lab at MSU.

A few million years of chaos, resolving into something that looks like a solar system. I love it — but it's a thing I do, not the whole of me, so that's all it gets here.
